One epoch, every week

01 · Open

Monday, NYSE open, fresh price. Deposits queue from here.

02 · Propose

The agent asks for a 0.20-delta call. The contract checks the mandate.

03 · Sell

Buyers pay USDG at Black-Scholes fair value from the live oracle, never below intrinsic value.

04 · Expire

Friday, 16:00 New York.

05 · Settle

First Chainlink print after expiry. Anyone can call it.

call in the money → (S − K) / S stock tokens per option

put in the money → K − S USDG per option

Collateral is locked per option sold, so the vault can never owe more than it holds.

Depositors get the premium in USDG every Friday. Deposits and withdrawals made during the week are processed at settlement, at the week's closing share price.
