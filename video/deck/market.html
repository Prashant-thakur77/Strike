Who pays, and how many

Supply

Stock-token holders

195 stock tokens are listed on Robinhood Chain. A covered call pays holders weekly premium in USDG for upside they sell.

Supply

USDG holders

A cash-secured put pays USDG holders to wait for a lower entry price.

Demand

Option buyers

Traders and holders hedging a position, paying USDG; buyer agents already do it through MCP.

Proof

A decades-old strategy

Covered-call writing is tracked by Cboe's BXM index with data back to 1986. Our 8-year backtest: 25–46% lower volatility than holding.

Sources: api.robinhood.com/rhj/assets (28 Sep 2026); cboe.com BXM; docs/backtest.md (TSLA, NVDA, AMZN, SPY, 2019–2026).

We do not quote a market-size number we cannot source. The demand is the holders of 195 listed stock tokens and the USDG on the chain, and the strategy is one of the oldest income strategies in equity markets.
