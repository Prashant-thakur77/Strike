Four traps in every stock-token integration

TrapWhat goes wrongWhat Strike does
Dividend multiplier (ERC-8056)Chainlink already includes it. Applying it again overprices NVDA by its live 1.000775 multiplier.Prices, strikes and payouts stay per raw token. The multiplier is never applied.
Weekend and holiday feedsTokens trade 24/7, but the feed freezes while NYSE is closed.Sell only in NYSE hours. Settle on the first print after expiry.
Two pause layersToken and oracle can both pause; testnet tokens lack oraclePaused().Every read checks both, defensively. Settlement waits.
Splits mid-epochFeed and multiplier can briefly disagree around the change.Sales pause; settlement moves to the first print after the change plus a grace period.
