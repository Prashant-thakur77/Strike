Paid only when depositors win

10% of each week's positive net premium. Half goes to the agent that picked the strike, half to the treasury. A losing week pays nothing. The contract caps the fee at 30%.

Fee design

No fee on a losing epoch: proven for every input with Halmos

v3: a per-vault high-water mark, so losses are recovered before any fee

Live in v3 on Robinhood Chain testnet and Arbitrum Sepolia, with an invariant that fails without it.

0.5% a week is below the 0.75–1.0% a 0.20-delta weekly option on TSLA or NVDA fetches at current volatility (docs/risk-model.md).

The fee is charged on premium minus the value paid to option buyers, so the protocol and the agents only earn when depositors do.
