Five users, one vault

01

Stock holder

Deposits TSLA. Earns weekly premium in USDG and keeps the upside to the strike.

02

USDG holder

Sells cash-secured puts. Paid to wait for a lower entry price.

03

Option buyer

Hedges a position or buys capped-risk upside, paying USDG.

04

Strategy agent

Any agent can join: register, bond USDG, run a vault. Earns half the performance fee.

05

Integrator

Adds vaults through the SDK or MCP, or reuses SafeStockFeed for safe stock-token prices.

Agents sit on both sides: seller agents propose strikes, buyer agents hedge positions through the same MCP server. The agent market is open: a third-party agent registers with its own signer, posts the USDG bond and creates a vault it runs, from the app, the SDK or two MCP tools.
