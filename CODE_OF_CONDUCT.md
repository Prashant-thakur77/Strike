# Code of Conduct

Strike follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and focus on the work. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not accepted in issues, pull requests, discussions or any project channel.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through the contact on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/Prashant-thakur77). Reports are handled confidentially, and the maintainer may remove content or block participants who break these rules.
