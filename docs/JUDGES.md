# Judge's tour

Strike is weekly options vaults for Robinhood Chain stock tokens, paid in USDG. An AI agent proposes each week's strike. The contract checks the proposal against the vault's immutable mandate, and if it breaks the rules, the agent's bond is slashed to depositors. v2 and v3 are live on Robinhood Chain testnet (46630) and v3 on Arbitrum Sepolia (421614). It is unaudited.

We are building Strike as a company on Robinhood Chain and Arbitrum, and these testnets are where it starts: the next steps are an external audit and a capped mainnet vault, and we keep the testnet vaults running every week after the buildathon either way. The plan, the business model and what funding buys are in the README's [Building Strike as a company](../README.md#building-strike-as-a-company). How Strike fits the buildathon's workshop themes (RWAs, agents, security, governance, the wedge) is in [the theme table](#how-strike-maps-to-the-workshop-themes).

## How Strike maps to the workshop themes

The buildathon's workshops covered Robinhood Chain, tokenized real-world assets, agents and payments, security, governance, product and the market. This is where Strike fits each theme, with what exists today. Status says what runs today: live (on the testnets or the deployed app), built but not deployed, prototype (on `main`, tested, not deployed), plan, or not applicable.

The wedge in one line: people who hold stock tokens on Robinhood Chain and want income from them. Stock tokens are real-world assets, and holding one earns the stock's return and nothing else. The options venues on the chain ask the holder to pick each strike or do not say who picks it ([Competition](../README.md#competition)); Strike gives the strike to a bonded agent, and what makes that hard to copy is the on-chain mandate: the contract checks every proposal and pays a rule-breaking agent's bond to depositors. Strike has no outside users yet.

## Finding your way in the app

The app nav has five places ([D53](decisions.md)): **Vaults** (the home), **Portfolio**, **Agents** (with every decision page), **Proof** (the proof page, the price monitor, lessons, governance, the Pendle design and the paid agent API) and **Learn** (the playground, the backtest, the glossary, Get started and the mainnet waitlist). On a phone they sit in a bottom tab bar, with More for the rest. [`/app`](https://strike-options.vercel.app/app) opens on three ways in: earn on your stock tokens, [try it without a wallet](https://strike-options.vercel.app/app/playground) and [see an AI decision](https://strike-options.vercel.app/app/decision/46630/2026-10-02-sTSLA-CSP-A2). On a vault page, "This week's call" sums up the agent's decision in five lines from its anchored record, then come deposit, this week's option, the timeline, your position and the mandate. "Why this strike", "Risk" and "Epoch trace" are folded under "Details for experts": open one with its summary, or all with "Open all", or follow a link ending in `#why`, `#risk` or `#trace`, which opens that section. The "Next" link at the foot of every page still walks the app in order.

## What works any time, and what needs NYSE hours

Two calls check the market: `EpochManager.openEpoch` and `buy` revert with `MarketClosed` outside an NYSE regular session ([`_requireMarketOpen`](../contracts/src/core/EpochManager.sol#L756), [`MarketCalendar.sol`](../contracts/src/oracle/MarketCalendar.sol)). Nothing else does. Judging falls mostly on weekends and Singapore evenings, when the market is closed, so the app states it: the vault page, the playground and the vault list say whether the market is open now and when it reopens, in UTC and in your own time zone, read from the deployed `MarketCalendar` (`isTradingDay`, `sessionOf`).

**Works any time**

- The mandate playground: `previewProposal` is a view with no market check. Its "Run the agent" section runs the example agent's rule stages (market checks, a ladder of strikes judged by the contract, the profile's pick, the critic) against a live vault in the browser and sends nothing.

## 15 minutes, with a clone

Needs Foundry, Node 22+ with pnpm, and Python 3 ([Quickstart](../README.md#quickstart) has the versions).

```bash
git clone --recursive https://github.com/Prashant-thakur77/Strike && cd Strike
pnpm install
scripts/demo-local.sh   # full epoch on anvil: accept, reject + slash, buy, settle, redeem
make test               # 607 Foundry tests
```

`scripts/demo-local.sh` takes about 15 seconds once the contracts are compiled. `SETTLE_PRICE=400 scripts/demo-local.sh` settles in the money instead. CI runs both.

No clone is needed to check the chain: [verify-yourself.md](verify-yourself.md) re-derives a buy, a rejection and its slash, a decision record's hash against its anchor, a mirrored price against mainnet Chainlink, the settlement price and a vault's mandate with `cast`, `jq` and `curl` only, no Strike code, each with the output we got.

Formal proofs ([formal-verification.md](security/formal-verification.md)): 9 properties proven with Halmos, 16 more written down and marked unproven.

```bash
pip install halmos==0.3.3
cd contracts
halmos --match-contract Formal --solver-timeout-assertion 300s
```

Fork tests: 16 tests against real Robinhood Chain mainnet TSLA, NVDA and SPY feeds, real USDG, the real ERC-8004 registries and Pendle's PT-USDG market ([RobinhoodFork.t.sol](../contracts/test/fork/RobinhoodFork.t.sol), [StockCollateralFork.t.sol](../contracts/test/fork/StockCollateralFork.t.sol), [PendleCollateralFork.t.sol](../contracts/test/fork/PendleCollateralFork.t.sol)).

```bash
ROBINHOOD_RPC_URL=https://rpc.mainnet.chain.robinhood.com forge test --root contracts --match-path "test/fork/*"
```

Backtest ([backtest.md](backtest.md)): 403 weeks on TSLA, NVDA, AMZN and SPY, 2019 to 2026, about 15 seconds. The 0.20-delta covered call lagged buy-and-hold on every ticker with 25–46% less volatility, and the report opens with that result.

```bash
python3 research/backtest.py
```

Internal security review ([review-2026-09-29.md](security/review-2026-09-29.md)): 11 findings (1 High, 3 Medium, 4 Low, 3 Info), all fixed. Each finding's test first reproduced the attack; the 19 tests now run as regression tests.

```bash
forge test --root contracts --match-path "test/audit/*" -vv
```

Admin timelock ([D43](decisions.md#d43--on-mainnet-the-admin-is-a-safe-behind-a-73-day-timelock-2026-10-02), [staged path](trust-model.md#staged-path-for-the-admin-keys)): on mainnet a feed, oracle or pricer change waits 73 days, longer than the longest epoch plus the settlement grace, so it cannot land inside a running epoch. 12 tests, including the worst case the contracts allow and a control that shows the swap landing under any shorter delay.

```bash
forge test --root contracts --match-path "test/governance/*"
```

Other numbers, from [testing.md](testing.md) and the README's [safety evidence](../README.md#safety-evidence): 99.3% line and 99.0% branch coverage (`make coverage`), 3 differential tests (Stylus against Solidity), 15 Rust tests, 590 TypeScript tests (SDK 282, MCP 96, example agents 159, indexer 53; 2 more SDK tests are opt-in live checks), 104 Telegram bot tests, 10 subgraph tests, 463 Playwright tests per viewport (430 run by default; 33 are the opt-in UI audit). The [threat model](threat-model.md) lists 25 threats, each with the test that covers it. Every count is in [`evidence/facts.json`](evidence/facts.json), and CI fails when this page states a different one.
