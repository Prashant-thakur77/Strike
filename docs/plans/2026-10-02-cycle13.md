Nothing here may break the two jobs that run today: agent #2's proposal at 14:07 UTC and the settlement of three epochs from 20:40 UTC. Changes to `scripts/keeper.sh`, `scripts/weekly-agent.sh` and `agents/example` are developed on a branch and merged only after the settlement has been logged.

## The mechanism (requirement 3)

The one thing a user still has to trust us about: on testnet, Strike's prices come from `MirrorFeed`s that our keeper fills with Robinhood Chain mainnet Chainlink rounds. A dishonest keeper could push a price the mainnet feed never printed and settle a vault at it. Candidates considered:

1. **Mirror audit (chosen):** every testnet price round is checked against the mainnet Chainlink round it claims to copy (same answer, same timestamp), by a script anyone can run and a live check in the app. It turns the last trusted input into a public check, and it is the input settlement depends on.
2. Admin-action audit: list every privileged call since deployment (pricer, spot buffer, roles, pause) and show "nothing changed". Useful, but the admin powers are already visible on Blockscout and none affects a settled payout directly.
3. Proof that the agent's reasoning is the model's: not possible to prove that a model produced a text; the anchored decision record already proves the text existed before the proposal.

The headline stays "an AI agent proposes, the contract decides"; the mirror audit closes the trust gap that headline leaves on testnet.
