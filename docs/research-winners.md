# Past winners, and what Strike took from them

Research done on 2026-09-30 from public sources (listed at the end). It covers past Arbitrum Open House winners, Stylus hackathon winners and other builds on Robinhood Chain, and asks what judges rewarded. The entries in this edition, the organiser updates of the last week and the final action list are in [research-final-week.md](research-final-week.md). Strike's submission closes on 2026-10-04 at 15:59 UTC, so each idea was sized to fit in four days or kept as roadmap. Where a claim comes only from a project's own HackQuest text or a search result, it is marked as unverified.

## Summary

There have been three Open House editions before Singapore: India (online buildathon and a Bengaluru hacker house), NYC and London. Each had an online buildathon followed by an in-person Founder House. No Open House Buenos Aires was found; Arbitrum's Buenos Aires event was ArbiVerse at Devconnect. Singapore is the fourth edition.

One of the judges has said what they look for. Ben (hummusonrails, Arbitrum DevRel and an Open House judge) wrote a guide for this Singapore buildathon, ["The AI wrote the code. You still have to defend it."](https://x.com/hummusonrails/status/2098064078438866979) It asks for:

- a README that lists what works and what does not;
- tests on the part you claim is hard, rather than a coverage percentage;
- a demo on a real network with one deliberate failure;
- the cuts and tradeoffs, with the reason for each;
- an answer to "why onchain, why Arbitrum, what would the Solidity version cost";
- a team that can explain any file a judge points at.

The NYC judging covered "technical execution, product clarity, ecosystem alignment, and the long-term potential". The winners "demonstrated clear user focus, promising architecture, and a clear and concise roadmap that extends beyond our Buildathon" ([Arbitrum Foundation blog](https://blog.arbitrum.foundation/open-house-nyc-buildathon-concludes-meet-the-winning-teams/)).

Established teams win often. London's online Open-category winners (Verus, Capricorn, Denaria) were existing products with users, and one reply to the announcement complained about exactly that ([thread](https://x.com/arbitrum/status/2069113415348744568)). Traction counts.

## Ideas taken from them

Each idea names its source and what Strike did with it.

1. A "what works, what does not yet, what we cut" block, from the judge guide ("Volunteering your gaps reads as command of the project. A judge discovering an unmentioned gap reads as either carelessness or concealment.") and ReineiraOS's "Scope and honesty" section. Done: [README](../README.md#what-works-what-does-not-yet-what-we-cut).
2. Lead the evidence with the tests that carry the claims, not totals. The guide: "coverage percentage is the wrong target… Test the part you claim is hard… a judge reading your test names learns what you believed could break." Done: the README's [claims table](../README.md#claims-and-the-tests-that-check-them).
3. A question-and-answer pack, so any file can be explained. The guide: "judges will open your submission assuming AI wrote some or even all of the code." Done: [submission/qa-prep.md](submission/qa-prep.md).
4. Stylus gas on the live chain, with the pricer cached, following Denaria, which measured on live Arbitrum Sepolia, isolated the WASM entry cost and published "Stylus does not automatically reduce gas". Done: the live measurement ([gas.md](gas.md#measured-on-the-live-chain)), and on Arbitrum Sepolia the v3 program is cached in ArbOS, measured cached and uncached ([gas.md](gas.md#v3-on-arbitrum-sepolia-uncached-and-cached)); caching is not available on Robinhood Chain testnet yet.
5. Agent onboarding by URL: a skill file, `llms.txt` and a remote MCP, following Tilt and AlphaGrid. Done on the app; publishing to npm and ClawHub is not done.
6. Anchor each agent decision on-chain, following Bastion and the ERC-8004 `feedbackURI` and `feedbackHash` fields. Done with `DecisionLog`, on all three deployments; passing the URI and hash in `giveFeedback` is pending (v3 still passes them empty).
7. Compare the vaults with USDG lending. Robinhood Earn shows about 7% estimated APY on USDG, and the Steakhouse USDG vault on Morpho paid about 1.9% on 2026-07-20 ([FalconX primer](https://x.com/FalconXGlobal/article/2079248025214407089), [Robinhood newsroom](https://robinhood.com/us/en/newsroom/robinhood-accelerates-global-expansion-robinhood-chain-mainnet-stock-tokens-agentic-trading/)). Agama and Saffron won Robinhood prizes by making stock tokens and LP positions productive. Done: the README's [comparison](../README.md#against-usdg-lending) and [research.md](research.md#usdg-lending-yields-the-benchmark-for-the-put-vault).
8. Quote Robinhood's own agent-trading language. Its Trading MCP lets users "connect their AI model of choice… with human-controlled capital allocation and safety parameters" ([newsroom](https://robinhood.com/us/en/newsroom/robinhood-accelerates-global-expansion-robinhood-chain-mainnet-stock-tokens-agentic-trading/)); FalconX counts 4,500+ Virtuals agents on RH. Done: README [Why only here](../README.md#why-only-here-robinhood-chain-and-arbitrum).
9. A milestone plan, following the NYC winners' "clear and concise roadmap that extends beyond our Buildathon". Singapore pays prizes 50% on milestones and grants are milestone-based. Done: [MILESTONES.md](MILESTONES.md).
10. Turn `SafeStockFeed` into a standard with a conformance suite, following ReineiraOS ("each shipping a semantic conformance suite") and EqualFi's ERC-8004 registration example. Done: [`contracts/test/conformance`](../contracts/test/conformance).
11. Judge-facing polish (a YouTube video, a hosted deck, security CI badges), following HarvestBot. CodeQL and gitleaks are done, and the narrated demo (5:13) and pitch (2:02) are in the repository; uploading them to YouTube and sharing the deck are pending (owner).
12. A "why only here" section. The guide: "A team that chose Stylus for a compute-heavy path and can say what the Solidity version would have cost them has made a decision." Done: README [Why only here](../README.md#why-only-here-robinhood-chain-and-arbitrum).
13. Traction mechanics, following The Risk Protocol's trading competition and AlphaGrid's "agents compete for capital". A Season 0 banner is on `/app/agents`; recruiting testers and outside agents is pending.
14. Mainnet presence, following EqualFi (RH testnet with 9 tokens), Saffron (production contracts on RH) and Lexifi (chain 4663, in this edition). A capped mainnet vault is milestone 3 in [MILESTONES.md](MILESTONES.md).

## Ideas that do not fit

| Idea (source)                                                                       | Why not for Strike                                                                                                       |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| FHE or confidential settlement (ReineiraOS, CanHav, Fangorn)                        | Strike's value is a public, checkable mandate and public pricing. Fhenix CoFHE support on Robinhood Chain is unverified. |
| Phylax Credible Layer assertions (Denaria `assertions/`)                            | Needs Phylax network support on chain 46630 (unverified). Strike has Halmos proofs, invariants and an internal review.   |
| Diamond proxy or upgradeable modules (EqualFi)                                      | Contradicts Strike's immutability decision (D13) and threat-model stance (T17).                                          |
| Gasless or paymaster UX (Verus)                                                     | Needs a bundler and paymaster on 46630. Kept as roadmap (a USDG paymaster).                                              |
| An own Orbit chain (GuardChain.ai)                                                  | No benefit; Robinhood Chain is the target.                                                                               |
| Off-chain pricing microservices (Laytus)                                            | On-chain Black-Scholes and a Stylus solver are what makes the mandate checkable.                                         |
| x402 pay-per-quote for agents (Fangorn, Pact, ReineiraOS)                           | Adds little to the judging criteria. ReineiraOS notes Arbitrum Sepolia has no hosted x402 facilitator. Roadmap at most.  |
| A Virtuals / GAME plugin for buyer agents                                           | Real distribution (4,500+ agents on RH), but too large for four days. Roadmap.                                           |
| Chasing test counts (EqualFi's "1,100+ tests")                                      | The judge guide says counts are the wrong target.                                                                        |
| A whitepaper PDF (Denaria) or a Mintlify docs site (AlphaGrid)                      | Little over the existing litepaper and judge's tour.                                                                     |
| Reusing the Stylus Rust math in the browser through wasm-bindgen (Renegade's reuse) | Needs a stylus-sdk-free build of `stylus/pricer/src/math.rs` and app wiring. Roadmap.                                    |
