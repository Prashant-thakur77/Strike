# Final-week research: the field, the organisers, and what to do

Research done on 2026-10-01, 19:00 to 20:00 UTC (2026-10-02 in Singapore), from public sources listed at the end. It covers three questions: who else is in this buildathon and where they overlap with Strike; what the organisers, Robinhood, Arbitrum and Paxos have published since 28 September; and what Strike should and should not do before submission closes on **2026-10-04 at 15:59 UTC**. It does not repeat [research.md](research.md) (chain facts, rules, the first competitor list) or [research-winners.md](research-winners.md) (past winners, the judge guide). Where a claim comes only from a project's own README or HackQuest text it says so; "not found" means it was looked for and not found, not that it does not exist.

## Summary

- **Strike is not on HackQuest yet.** The API list of submitted projects (52 visible) does not include it, and no project page exists under `Strike`, `strike` or `Strike-Options`. Registration closes **2026-10-02 at 17:01 UTC**, about 21 hours after this was written. Nothing else in this file matters if that is missed.
- The field has grown from 88 projects on 28 September to **141** (HackQuest `projectCount`), with **1,103+ participants**. 52 submitted projects are visible through the API; at least four more are submitted but hidden from the list (HarvestBot, AfterHours, Vigiles, Afterglow), so the visible list is not complete.
- **Three other options projects on Robinhood Chain stock tokens now exist**, none of them an agent-run vault: AfterHours (weekend puts, Stylus pricer, ERC-4626 writer vaults, submitted), Novation (portfolio-margined weekly options clearinghouse with a Stylus risk kernel, repo created 1 October, submission not confirmed) and CallPut (live on mainnet with 110 verified contracts, not confirmed as an entrant). Strike's bonded-agent mandate, slashing to depositors, ERC-8004 identity and DecisionLog remain unique in this field. HarvestBot is the only other entry with "an onchain mandate and a slashable bond", for tax-loss harvesting, not options.
- **The weekend-gap story is crowded.** AfterHours (two projects), Afterglow, Vigil, batpilot, Novation and Nokturn all open with "tokens trade 24/7, feeds run 24/5". Strike handles it (settle on the first print after expiry) but should not re-pitch around it.
- **No judging process beyond the form was found.** The organiser calendar lists nothing after the 4 October deadline: no demo day, Q&A or office hours. HackQuest's in-platform judging is switched off (`disableJudge: true`), so the Foundation judges from the submission, the repository and the video. No judges are named anywhere. The last feedback session was 28 September.
- **Robinhood's HOOD Summit (29–30 September) gave Strike its best adoption numbers**: "over 150,000 customers have opened agentic trading accounts and now, agents use Robinhood's tools almost 30 million times a day", plus Agent Apps that sell options data to agents and a Legend desktop that runs third-party agents over the Trading MCP. Weekend equities trading is "coming early next year, pending regulatory review".
- **Pendle listed a fixed-yield USDG market on Robinhood Chain on 30 September**: PT-USDG, March 2027, 3.45% implied APY on 2 October. It is a cleaner benchmark for the put vault than the two numbers the README uses now.
- **Nothing changed in the infrastructure Strike depends on**: no new ERC-8004 registry code since 15 September, no Stylus CacheManager on Robinhood Chain testnet or mainnet (checked on-chain), no Chainlink testnet feed file, the same five faucet stock tokens on testnet, NFLX still without a mainnet feed. The Arbitrum Security Program (28 September, $7.8M, subsidised audits) is new and fits Strike's audit milestone.

## 4. What not to do

- **Do not redeploy any contract or change an address** before the form is filed. Every address in the form must match Blockscout, and v2's live epoch on Robinhood Chain and both v3 epochs settle on 2 October; a redeploy breaks the proof page and the epoch logs.
- **Do not re-pitch Strike as a weekend-gap product.** Seven entries already are, two of them with Stylus pricers for the gap. Strike's claim is the enforced mandate.
- **Do not build portfolio margin, spreads, an order book or a clearinghouse** to answer Novation or CallPut. Strike is a vault with one weekly series; its scope is why the mandate can be checked.
- **Do not add x402, FHE, a Virtuals plugin or a new chain.** [research-winners.md](research-winners.md#ideas-that-do-not-fit) already rules these out and nothing this week changes that.
- **Do not inflate counts or claims.** The judge guide warns against it and HarvestBot's README shows how a "fair" number next to a headline number reads better than the headline alone. Keep "unaudited", "testnet" and "mock feed on testnet" in every summary.
- **Do not claim mainnet.** CallPut, Stonkhouse, AfterHours Oracle, Nokturn and Pippin's Hollow are on mainnet; Strike's mainnet vault is milestone 3. A capped mainnet deploy in the last 48 hours without the audit would contradict the roadmap and the decisions log.
- **Do not copy competitor features** (Vigiles' dead-man switch, Novation's session multipliers, AfterHours' closed-time vol). Reference, do not import; judges will have read those repos too.
- **Do not spend the last day on npm or ClawHub publishing.** No judging step reads a registry; the remote MCP and `/skill.md` are already on the app.
