# Internal security review, 2026-09-29

Scope: every file in `contracts/src` (focus on `EpochManager`, `StrikeVault`, `AgentRegistry`, `FeeManager`, `SafeStockFeed`, `StockOracle`, `MandateGuard`, `Decimals`, `BlackScholesLib`), checked against [design.md](../design.md) and [threat-model.md](../threat-model.md). Review method: an adversarial manual read, with a Foundry test for every claim. No source file or existing test was changed during the review; the fixes followed the same day (see Status).

Evidence lives in `contracts/test/audit/`:

| File                    | Content                                                                   |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `AuditSettlement.t.sol` | Settlement round selection, corporate-action and phase liveness, guardian |
| `AuditPricing.t.sol`    | Sale-time pricing, proposals, mandates, keeper sigma                      |
| `AuditVault.t.sol`      | Vault queue, accumulator, rounding, reentrancy, ERC-4626 behaviour        |

Each finding's test first reproduced the attack and failed. After the fixes below, every one of them asserts the fixed behaviour and passes, so they now run in `make test` as regression tests:

```bash
forge test --root contracts --match-path "test/audit/*" -vv   # 19 passing
```

## Findings

| ID   | Severity | Title                                                                                  | Impact                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Test                                                                                                   | Suggested fix                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |

| H-01 | High     | A corporate action near expiry makes settlement permanently impossible                 | `SafeStockFeed.settlementPrice` refuses the one fixed "first round after expiry" if it lies within `corporateActionGrace` of `effectiveAt`. Later rounds fail the predecessor check, so no round is ever accepted. design.md §10 promises settlement "pauses until effectiveAt + grace". Instead the vault stays locked for `settlementGrace` (7 days) and then only `emergencyCancel` is left: in-the-money buyers get their premium back and lose the payout (the test shows a 26.49 USDG refund against 4.17 TSLA owed), and when the options expire worthless depositors lose the premium. Quarterly ERC-8056 dividend multipliers make this recur. | `test_AUDIT_corporateActionBricksSettlement`                                                           | When the first print after expiry is inside the window, move the target to `effectiveAt + grace` and require the first round at or after it, checked against its predecessor. Record that price for every vault on the (token, expiry). |
| M-01 | Medium   | First-round-of-phase fallback: a caller can choose the price, or settlement bricks     | (a) When a Chainlink aggregator upgrade happens within `maxPriceAge` (25 h) after expiry, the true first round in the old phase and round 1 of the new phase both pass. The first caller picks the price (260 vs 300 in the test), which breaks T1. (b) When the phase changes over a weekend and the old phase has no round after the close, round 1 of the new phase is more than `maxPriceAge` late, so every round is rejected: the same lock-then-cancel outcome as H-01.                                                                                                                                                                          | `test_AUDIT_phaseChangeLetsCallerPickSettlementPrice`, `test_AUDIT_weekendPhaseChangeBricksSettlement` | For round 1 of phase `p`, also take a hint for the last round of phase `p-1` and verify that it predates expiry. Drop the `maxPriceAge` window, or fall back to it only when phase `p-1` is provably empty after the target.            |
| M-02 | Medium   | Sales continue in the money, below intrinsic value                                     | `buy` charges `fairValue × premiumBps`. The reference mandate lets `premiumBps` go down to 9,500, and the example agent clamps to that. Once the call is deep in the money near expiry, anyone buys it at 95% of intrinsic value, hedges the delta of about 1 and takes a riskless profit from depositors. The test buys 60 options for 1,425 USDG and gets 1,500 USDG back.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | `test_AUDIT_itmSalesBelowIntrinsic`                                                                    | Charge at least intrinsic value: `max(fair × bps, intrinsic)`. Or stop sales once the strike is on the wrong side of spot (re-run the `StrikeWrongSide` rule in `buy`).                                                                 |
| M-03 | Medium   | Oracle-latency arbitrage inside the 0.5% deviation band                                | Mainnet feeds only print on a 0.5% move or a 24 h heartbeat, and `buy` prices off the last print. For the short-dated, low-delta options the mandate targets, a 0.49% real move gives the buyer an edge of 23% of the premium, even at `premiumBps = 10,000`. The premium factor does not cover this lag.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | `test_AUDIT_oracleDeviationBandArbitrage`                                                              | Price buys at the spot shifted against the buyer by the feed's deviation threshold (calls: `S × 1.005`, puts: `S × 0.995`). Alternatively, use a low-latency pull oracle (Data Streams) and require a print only seconds old.           |
| L-01 | Low      | `proposeByDelta` rounding gets honest agents slashed                                   | The solved strike is rounded down to a cent. For a call that raises the delta and for a put it lowers it. A target on the band edge (for example `maxDeltaBps`) then falls outside the band: the proposal is rejected, 25 USDG is slashed and the agent gets a strike. The test hits this at spot 250.00. This breaks the documented promise of "exactly the delta the agent intended".                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | `test_AUDIT_proposeByDeltaRoundingSlashesAgent`                                                        | Round away from spot, toward the band's interior (calls up, puts down, then clamp to the band). Or revert rather than slash when the rejection comes only from a strike the contract derived.                                           |
| L-02 | Low      | Market-data races slash honest proposals                                               | A rejection slashes rather than reverts, and a proposal carries no bound on the spot or sigma it assumed. A keeper can also do this on purpose.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | `test_AUDIT_sigmaUpdateRaceSlashesHonestAgent`                                                         | Add `expectedSigma` and `maxSpotDeviation` (or a `deadline` plus a spot band) to the proposal. Also reviewed and held, with no separate test because the existing suite already covers them:

- Call payout < collateral, put payout ≤ collateral, and per-holder floor rounding ≤ aggregate escrow.
- FeeManager `agentCut ≤ fee`; `creditFees` cannot over-credit.
- Slashing reaches unbonding funds.
- Only the manager can mint or burn options and lock or settle vaults; the manager is set once; clones cannot be re-initialised.
- `settle`, `redeem` and `abortEpoch` still work while the guardian has paused the manager.
- No unbounded loops on the settlement path; the reputation registry is wrapped in `try`/`catch`.

Considered and dropped as not exploitable:

- **Inflation or first-depositor attack.** Accounting is internal, so the share price can only fall through payouts.
- **Gas-griefing the ERC-8004 `try`/`catch`.** The 1/64 remainder is too small for the code that runs after it.
- **Curator front-running.** A curator cannot change anything `_evaluate` reads while the vault is locked.
- **Self-slashing into your own vault.** This only recovers the agent's own bond; strikes still accrue.
