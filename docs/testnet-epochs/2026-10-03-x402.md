# Paid risk reports over x402, 2026-10-03

The first paid calls to `GET /api/agent/risk-report` ([ENDPOINTS.md](../ENDPOINTS.md#paid-route-x402)), on Saturday 3 October from 17:02 to 17:20 UTC. An AI agent (the [example agent](../../agents/example/README.md) with `--paid-report`, through the MCP tool `paid_risk_report`) asked for a vault's risk report, got 402 Payment Required with the price, signed an EIP-3009 authorization for exactly 0.01 of a stablecoin, and got the report back with the settlement transaction. Two payments settled on Arbitrum Sepolia in Circle's test USDC and one on Robinhood Chain testnet in Paxos USDG. Why it is built this way is [D48](../decisions.md).

The app ran locally from the `x402` branch (`next start` on the production build) with the relayer key in `X402_RELAYER_KEY`, because the key is not yet set on Vercel; the chains, tokens, payer and payee are the real ones, and every transaction below is on-chain.

Each settlement (5 to 7) is a call to the token's `transferWithAuthorization` from the relayer. Its receipt carries the token's `AuthorizationUsed` event for the payer and the nonce the payer signed, and a `Transfer` of 10,000 units (0.01 at 6 decimals) from the payer to the payee. The payer's balances read before and after: 880,198 to 870,198 USDC units on Arbitrum Sepolia after 5, and 7,000,000 to 6,990,000 USDG units on Robinhood Chain testnet after 6. The payer paid no gas for any of them.

Circle's [faucet](https://faucet.circle.com) is a web form behind reCAPTCHA, so an agent cannot use it; transaction 4 got test USDC from Uniswap's testnet pool instead.

## The run

```bash
STRIKE_CHAIN_ID=421614 STRIKE_X402_MAX_SPEND=0.02 \
  STRIKE_X402_ENDPOINT=http://127.0.0.1:3292/api/agent/risk-report \
  STRIKE_PAYER_KEY=<team wallet key> \
  pnpm --filter @strike/agent-example start -- \
  --vault 0x5655659E18bf54ee0EF8f6A816E2e18D000F7311 --dry-run --ignore-session --paid-report --log <dir>
```

The decision record the run wrote has the payment under `payments` and a "Paid data (x402)" section: what was bought, the amount and token, the payee, the endpoint and the settlement link. The rest of the run stopped as it should on a dry run that day: the vault's series had expired and was waiting for its settlement, so there was nothing to propose. Run 6 was the same command with `STRIKE_CHAIN_ID=46630` on the v2 vault `0xADFF7900dbe01E8170a750AB88e1f4eA8D9D1D4e`, and the payer chose USDG because the route accepts it on the agent's chain.

The report paid for in 7, for the v3 put vault on Arbitrum Sepolia, answered with every part: the vault and its mandate, the series risk (none that day: the epoch was idle, which the report says), the settlement audit (`ok: true`), the price mirror audit of TSLA (17 rounds checked, 17 matched against Robinhood Chain mainnet Chainlink), the anchored decision record of epoch 1 with its hash and DecisionLog transaction, and links to the vault page and the epoch trace.
