## Not tested, and why

- Buying an option and opening an epoch: both revert outside NYSE hours, and the market was closed all weekend. The buy panel was checked in its closed state only.
- Settlement, claims and redeeming options: the 2 October series wait for Monday's first mainnet print.
- Registering an agent: the 50 USDG bond is more than the faucet gives a new wallet.
- Real wallet extensions: the injected wallet answers the same EIP-1193 calls (`eth_requestAccounts`, `wallet_switchEthereumChain`, `eth_sendTransaction`), but MetaMask's own add-network and switch prompts were not seen.
- The fixes on the live site. Vercel answered the pushes from 09:19 UTC on with "Deployment rate limited, retry in 24 hours" (the commit status of `763a21a`, `84af277` and `da544dd`), so at 10:15 UTC the site still ran `083f5f8`, without B1 to B7; rows 16 and 17 are the deposit flow re-run there. Every fix was checked instead on a production build (`next build`, `next start`) reading the live testnets, with the specs named above on both viewports and the whole Playwright suite. Re-run the flows once Vercel deploys again.
- For about ten minutes after the mobile audit (about 80 page loads with their RPC reads) Vercel answered this machine with its "Security Checkpoint" (HTTP 403); the live smoke test from GitHub Actions passed at the same time.
