## Friday: agent #2 proposes with the conservative profile

The example agent gained `--profile conservative` for this: 0.15 delta (agent #1 sells 0.20), priced at 108% of Black-Scholes fair value, offering at most half the vault's capacity. The plan is still clamped into the vault's mandate, dry-run with `risk_check` (the contract's own `previewProposal`) and only then sent; the decision record names the planner `rule: conservative` ([agents/example/src/strategy.ts](../../agents/example/src/strategy.ts), six tests in `strategy.test.ts` and `record.test.ts`).

### The command

From the repo root, after the NYSE opens on Friday 2 October (13:30 UTC; the keeper's hourly mirror keeps the TSLA feed within its 25-hour `maxPriceAge`). The key is read from the key file into the environment and never printed:


## Notes from the run

- **What the app does not offer.** Minting an ERC-8004 identity (step 1; the form links an existing one) and naming the vault. Everything else went through the app's own forms and signatures.
- **Rehearsed on a fork first.** The signing scene and the vault driver were rehearsed against an anvil fork of the testnet (`SIGN_RPC`, `video/.out/fork.sh`) before the one-shot live run. Two findings were fork artefacts (anvil's 1 gwei base fee made the 0.0005 ETH wallet "unable to pay" at 200k gas, and `estimateGas` through the fork failed once with "An unknown RPC error occurred"), neither of which exists on the live chain (0.012 gwei fee cap, zero priority fee).
- **The deposit took two takes.** In the first take the app's "Approve & deposit" sent the approval (transaction 6) and showed the deposit's signature request, but the recorder's Confirm press did not land on the panel and the driver sat on it; the same sequence works in a leaner driver, and the live deposit was then recorded with it (transaction 7, `agent2-deposit.mp4`). The scene code now checks that each Confirm press registered and presses the panel directly if not.
- **App nit.** Right after a successful registration the form re-validates the signer field and shows "already signs for agent 2, use a fresh key" in red under the green "registered and bonded" message. Harmless, briefly visible in `signing.mp4` around 20 s.
- **README.** `video/lib/facts.mjs` read the review-issue count from "found N issues and all are fixed; that is not an audit"; the sentence grew a second review clause on 1 October, so the pattern now stops at "all are fixed".
