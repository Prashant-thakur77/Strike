# Friday settlement, rehearsed on forks (2026-10-01)

The three live series expire on Friday 2026-10-02 at 20:00 UTC (`expiry` 1790971200): the v2 TSLA covered call on Robinhood Chain testnet ([log](2026-09-29.md)), the v3 covered call next to it ([log](2026-09-30-v3.md#7-live-epoch-1-october)) and the v3 covered call on Arbitrum Sepolia ([log](2026-09-30-arbitrum-sepolia.md#6-the-epoch-1729-to-1734-utc-nyse-open)). On Thursday 1 October, 19:40 to 20:05 UTC, the whole of Friday was run twice on local anvil forks of both chains, once with TSLA settling in the money at $380 and once out of the money at $350. Nothing was sent to a public chain. The runbook that came out of it is in [operations.md](../operations.md#friday-2-october-settlement-runbook).

## Setup

- `anvil --fork-url <public RPC> --chain-id <id> --auto-impersonate`, forked at the head: 46630 at block 127,274,001 and 421614 at block 314,739,950 (19:40 UTC). Every sender was impersonated: the deployer `0x26b2…13Ff` (keeper role, curator, agent owner, v2 buyer), the two v3 buyers, a TSLA holder found in the token's transfer logs. No key file was read. The keeper itself ran with a throwaway key generated for the forks (`0xb1D7…6aaA`), funded with `anvil_setBalance` and given `KEEPER_ROLE` on the fork feeds; `settle` is permissionless, so the role only matters for the mirror step.
- Before the time warp: 1 TSLA was queued for deposit and 1 share-TSLA for redemption on each locked covered-call vault (`requestDeposit`, `requestRedeem`), so the queues had something to process. Agent #1's signer was rotated to the throwaway on all three registries (`setSigner`, on v3 with the throwaway's EIP-712 `SetSigner` consent sent by the owner), so the example agent's `--settle --log --anchor` could sign `DecisionLog.record` without the real signer key.
- `evm_snapshot`, then `evm_setNextBlockTimestamp 1790971500` + `evm_mine`: chain time 2026-10-02T20:05:00Z. The deployer pushed the TSLA MirrorFeed round `push(38000000000, 1790971260)` (380.00 at 20:01:00 UTC, 8 decimals): round 15 on 46630 (shared by v2 and v3), round 10 on 421614. For the second pass the forks were reverted to the snapshot and `35000000000` was pushed instead.
- Then `scripts/keeper.sh --once --dry-run` and `--once` with `RPC_URL` pointing at the fork, followed by the redeems, aborts, claims and the agent's record runs.

## What went wrong, and what changed

1. **The keeper compared expiry with the machine's clock.** `settle_expired` used `date -u +%s`, so on the warped fork the dry run listed every vault as "selling, expires 2026-10-02T20:00:00Z" while the chain was past expiry. `settle()` checks `block.timestamp`, so `scripts/keeper.sh` now reads the latest block's timestamp. Live chains are within seconds of the wall clock; forks and devnets are not.
2. **v2's ERC-8004 feedback was not posted.** The first keeper run settled v2 with `ReputationFeedback(1, 114, −30554055, "strike.epoch.pnl", posted = false)` while both v3 settlements posted. `cast run` on the transaction shows the official Reputation Registry reverting inside `AgentRegistry._feedback`'s `try/catch` with an out-of-gas sentry: `cast send` had used `eth_estimateGas` as the limit (702,223; the transaction used 683,023), and an estimate is the lowest gas at which the outer call does not revert, which a caught inner failure still is. The v2 registry had never posted feedback before, so its `giveFeedback` (a new client, more storage writes: 115,694 gas before it died) needed more than the 63/64 slack; the v3 registries, known clients since their slashes, needed 76,564 and fitted. The keeper now sends `settle` with `cast estimate` × 1.5; the out-of-the-money pass with that change posted v2's feedback. The SDK's `execute` (the MCP's `settle_epoch`, the agent's `--settle`) already uses estimate × 1.25. A settlement by hand should pass `--gas-limit 1200000`. Feedback is only posted inside `settle`, so a missed one cannot be posted later (the on-chain `track(1)` is still updated).
3. **A failed mirror push was logged as "mirrored".** After the forks were reverted (which dropped the throwaway's `KEEPER_ROLE`), the AMD push reverted `AccessControlUnauthorizedAccount` and the keeper still printed "AMD mirrored": `set -e` is suspended inside `mirror_prices || echo "mirror failed"`. The branches are now explicit, and one feed's failure no longer hides behind the others.
4. **The mainnet RPC rate-limits.** A read-only walk over the mainnet Chainlink TSLA feed's rounds got a Cloudflare 403 from `rpc.mainnet.chain.robinhood.com`, and the keeper's concurrent read came back empty, which showed as `[: : integer expected`. An empty read of either feed now skips the symbol with a message instead.
5. **Anvil forks of the Robinhood testnet go stale within the hour.** The public RPC keeps no archive state; about 25 minutes after forking, reads of accounts the fork had not yet touched failed with "historical state … is not available". Fork right before a run, and touch what you need early.
6. **The brief's expiry was off by eight hours**: 1,791,000,000 is 2026-10-03T04:00Z. The series' `expiry` is 1,790,971,200, Friday 20:00 UTC, which is what the forks were warped past.
7. **The mainnet feed prints about every 24 minutes.** The six TSLA rounds readable on mainnet today spanned 16:04 to 19:55 UTC with a median gap of 1,441 s and a longest of 5,972 s (the feed is deviation-triggered). The first print at or after 20:00:00 UTC may come well after the close, and only the rounds the keeper happens to mirror reach the testnet feed: the testnet settlement price is the first mirrored mainnet print after the close.
8. **The settlement record anchors over the proposal.** `anchorEpoch("settle", …)` returns the vault's current epoch, which is still 1 after settlement (it advances at the next `lock`), so the agent's `--settle --anchor` wrote `latestHash(1, vault, 1)` over the proposal's hash (on the live v3 46630 DecisionLog that is `0xa68cb230…` today). The app's "Why this strike" checks a record against `latestHash(agentId, vault, epoch)`, so after Friday's `agent.yml` settle run (which passes `--log --anchor`) the proposal record would read "does not match". The `DecisionRecorded` events keep both hashes. This needs a decision, not a script change: record Friday's settlement with `--log` and no `--anchor`, change `anchorEpoch` for settle records, or have the app check the events. Resolved on 1 October by the last option: the app checks each record against its own anchoring transaction's event, so settlement records are anchored (operations.md step 7).
9. **Smaller notes.** `MissingHint` cannot occur on a MirrorFeed: its round ids have no phase, and `recordSettlementPriceWithHints(TSLA, 1790971200, [10])` returned the price on the fork. `settle(vault, 9)` with the last pre-expiry round and `settle(vault, 11)` with the second post-expiry round both reverted `InvalidSettlementRound` (`0xc0b00676`), and the keeper's walk-back chose round 10 of the two. The keeper's dry run shows "no print after expiry yet" until a post-close round is on the testnet feed. `agent.yml` covers 46630's v2 vaults file only, so the v3 records on 46630 and on 421614 are run by hand with the SDK overrides, and v2's and v3's records on 46630 land in the same folder with the same base name (the second gets `-2`).

Fork transaction hashes are not listed: they exist only on the forks, which were stopped at the end.
