# Threat model

Strike is unaudited. This document lists what can go wrong, what the contracts do about it, and the test that proves it. Trust assumptions are stated plainly at the end.

## Assets at risk

- Collateral in vaults: stock tokens (call vaults) and USDG (put vaults)
- Premium escrowed in the `EpochManager` during an epoch, and premium owed to depositors in each vault
- Payouts owed to option holders after settlement
- Agent bonds in the `AgentRegistry`

## Trust assumptions

- **Chainlink** publishes correct stock prices per raw token, including the ERC-8056 multiplier (verified on chain 4663, see fork tests).
- **Robinhood** (token issuer) can pause tokens and oracles and change multipliers; Strike waits rather than acts during those states.
- **Admin** lists tokens and feeds, sets volatility bounds, the spot buffer (at most 200 bps) and fee parameters within hard caps, and can pause new activity. It has no function that moves user funds, but before a settlement price is recorded it can choose it by pointing the token at another feed (`StockOracle.setFeed`) or swapping the oracle (`EpochManager.setOracle`), and `setPricer` sets sale prices (T17). So "cannot move user funds" holds only for an honest admin; a recorded price is final. On the testnets the [price mirror audit](trust-model.md) detects such a swap but does not prevent it.
- **Guardian** can pause and, only after `settlementGrace` with no settlement price recorded for the series, cancel it (refund path). It cannot move funds anywhere but back to depositors and buyers.
- **Keeper** moves implied volatility within admin bounds, by at most 25% per update and at most once an hour. Sigma sets the price of live series, so a keeper can still move sale prices within those limits; it cannot change the verdict on a proposal made in an open epoch. On testnets the keeper also mirrors mainnet prices into `MirrorFeed`s.
- **Agent signer keys** consent (EIP-712) to the owner they serve; once a consent is used, the binding lasts until the owner rotates the key. A signer should sign only for an owner it trusts, and (on the branch) withdraw an unused consent with `revokeConsents`.
- **USDG** is treated as exactly $1.

## Out of scope for v1

- Early exercise and secondary markets for options (ERC-1155 positions are transferable, but there is no built-in book)
- Multi-series epochs and spreads
- Gas sponsorship (paymaster)
