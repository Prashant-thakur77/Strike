# Deck outline (16 slides, YC format)

The deck itself is shared separately (PPTX or PDF). It follows the order a YC partner reads a deck in: what it is, the problem, why now, the product, proof it works, who pays, who else is there, how it makes money, why to trust it, and the ask. Every figure on a slide has its source on the slide or in the speaker notes.
