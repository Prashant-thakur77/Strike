# Changelog

All notable changes to Strike. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/) (pre-1.0: a minor version may change contract interfaces). Deployed contracts are immutable; a contract change means a new deployment, listed under **Deployments**.

## [1.2.0] - 2026-10-03

Payments, onboarding and governance: agents pay per call over x402, a new wallet gets starter gas, the governance page reads every admin key from the chain, wallets get statements, alerts and a portfolio dashboard, the app has five nav places, and a Pendle collateral prototype is fork-tested. ## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-03

The agent as a pipeline of specialists, a decision page for every record, the keeper and weekly agent on GitHub Actions, and checks that keep every cited transaction and stated count true.

### Added

- Agent: the example agent is a pipeline of specialists ([D45](docs/decisions.md), [how](agents/example/README.md#specialists)). The market analyst checks the oracle's status and age against `maxPriceAge`, the NYSE session and next open from the `MarketCalendar`, a pending ERC-8056 corporate action, the sequencer feed, and sigma against its bounds and against realised volatility from mainnet Chainlink closes, and says go or no-go. The risk analyst dry-runs a ladder of target deltas through `risk_check` (the contract's `previewProposal`) with the risk engine's greeks and ±30% stress, the break-even and the model odds of exercise. The strike planner picks a rung by the profile's rule, or Claude does with read-only tools and the analysts' numbers. The critic may adjust the plan inside the mandate and records each change, then applies five rules in priority order (P0 market, P1 mandate, P2 break-even cushion, P3 drift, P4 yield), each with a reason code, the measured value and the threshold, and returns PASS, MODIFY or FAIL; a mandate or cushion veto gets one retry a rung further out. The contract's preflight is the last stage. A no-go or a veto is a no-trade record with reason codes, anchored like a proposal. Each record carries `decision.pipeline` (every stage's inputs, output, verdict, sources, duration and who ran it; a stage not run with its reason), the alternatives to grade at settlement (the chosen strike, keeping cash, half size, a rung either side), a computed confidence (the model odds that the option expires worthless) and the contradictions found between stages; Claude's text is kept as narration apart from the numbers. `--ignore-session` for dry runs. The 3 October dry runs on the v3 put vaults of both chains, one planned by Claude, are in [`docs/agent-log/dry-runs`](docs/agent-log/dry-runs).
- Agent: with `--llm`, the record keeps what the Claude call used as `decision.llm`, exactly as the source reported it: calls, input, output and cache tokens, Claude Code's list-price estimate (the API reports no price, so it is recorded as not provided) and time. The decision page's run line shows it ([agent README](agents/example/README.md#specialists)).
- Agent: decision records list the alternatives the agent dry-ran (`decision.candidates`): a ladder of target deltas across the mandate's band and a little past each edge, each with its strike, fair value, premium, yield, size and the contract's verdict, and for a rejection the first rule it fails with the measured value and the limit.
- Agent: a settle run with nothing to settle yet sends nothing and writes no record. Before expiry it exits 0; after expiry, while the price feed has no round at or after expiry, it exits 75, which `scripts/weekly-agent.sh` and `agent.yml` report as waiting, not as a failed run. A vault whose newest record is a "skipped" settle record from the last 7 days is skipped ([`settle-recorded.jq`](scripts/settle-recorded.jq)).
- App: a decision page for every agent record, `/app/decision/<chain>/<record>` (v2 and v3, both chains, accepted, rejected and no-trade; `?dry=1` opens a pipeline dry run). Its sections: the specialists as a stage strip (each stage's verdict, time and who ran it), what it chose and why, the mandate scorecard (each rule in `MandateGuard.check` order with its limit and headroom), why not the other strikes (the agent's own dry runs, or the ladder recomputed with the SDK from the record's snapshot), what would make the week lose (break-even, the model odds of exercise, stress rows), in hindsight (the price path from the mirrored feed rounds, each checked against mainnet Chainlink), what if (the proposal against keeping cash, half size and the rungs either side, as model numbers before settlement and graded at the settlement price after), the anchor and its transactions, and consistency checks between the record's numbers. Every section names its source. Linked from "Why this strike", the decision log and the epoch trace.
- App: [`/app/lessons`](https://strike-options.vercel.app/app/lessons), nine things that went wrong in the live runs and what changed because of them ([`lessons.json`](docs/evidence/lessons.json)), each with its evidence, the commit and the test that guards the change; `check-links.mjs` checks the paths and commits and `check-claims.mjs` the transactions.
- App: [`/app/portfolio`](https://strike-options.vercel.app/app/portfolio), one connected wallet across every vault, read from the chain: shares and their value in the vault's asset and in USD, premium to claim, queued and claimable deposits and withdrawals, and every option of these vaults it holds with where it stands.
- App: "Run the agent" in the playground. The example agent's rule stages run in the browser against a live Robinhood Chain testnet vault (the market checks, a ladder of target deltas each judged by the contract's `previewProposal`, the profile's pick, the critic) and show in the decision page's stage strip; nothing is sent.
- App: the agents page adds live series health (each live series against its strike and break-even, the time left and the model odds of exercise, with alerts from the same reads), per-agent performance from `AgentRegistry.track` with what a sample that size can say, a depositor PnL column on the leaderboard, the runs that sold nothing, the specialist pipeline's latest dry runs, a week-by-week view (proposals and verdicts, USDG slashed, runs that did not send, settlements and their premium; no profile recommendation below five settled epochs), and decision-log filters by kind and by text.
- App: "Running by itself" at the top of `/app/proof`, from `GET /api/status`: per testnet each MirrorFeed's latest round against mainnet's latest Chainlink print with the oracle's `maxPriceAge`, the market state, each vault's epoch and last settlement or abort, and whether `keeper.yml` and `agent.yml` are switched on and how their last runs ended.
- App: the epoch trace on the vault page, with `GET /api/epoch-trace` and `GET /api/settlement-audit`: every step of the vault's current and previous epoch in chain order, each with its transaction and evidence, and the settlement round checked against mainnet Chainlink.
- App: market hours where they matter. `openEpoch` and `buy` revert outside an NYSE session, so the buy panel, the idle vault, the playground, the vault list and a new vault's success note say whether the market is open and when it reopens (UTC, the viewer's time zone and a countdown), read from the deployed `MarketCalendar`.

- RPC: Alchemy for every Strike process when `ALCHEMY_API_KEY` is set, with the public RPCs as the fallback ([D39](docs/decisions.md)). The app gets `POST /api/rpc/<chainId>` (46630, 421614, 4663; [`route.ts`](app/src/app/api/rpc/%5BchainId%5D/route.ts), core in [`lib/rpc/proxy.ts`](app/src/lib/rpc/proxy.ts)): the key stays in the server environment and goes to Alchemy in an `Authorization` header; 15 read methods only (no `eth_sendRawTransaction`; wallets send their own transactions), batches up to 100 calls, bodies up to 256 kB, a 30-second per-instance cache for `eth_call` and `eth_getLogs` pinned to a block (never `latest`), per-call fallback to the public RPC when Alchemy is down, rate-limits or refuses a call (the free tier's 10-block `eth_getLogs` limit), never past a revert, a 30-second skip for an upstream that timed out, refused the key, rate-limited or failed with a 5xx (a refused log range does not count), upstream answers capped at 4 MB with a response-size error the log scans halve on, the key scrubbed from anything relayed, and no CORS headers. `GET` says which provider answers, shown as "RPC: Alchemy" or "RPC: public" in `/app/proof`'s live pricer card. ## [0.6.0] - 2026-09-29

### Security

- Internal adversarial review of every contract: 1 High, 3 Medium, 4 Low, 3 Info, each shown by a failing test. All fixed; the 19 audit tests now run as regression tests ([review](docs/security/review-2026-09-29.md)).
  - H-01: a corporate action near expiry could block settlement. Settlement now moves to the first print after `effectiveAt + grace`.
  - M-01: Chainlink phase changes. The previous phase's last round is required as proof, with no age window.
  - M-02: premium is never below intrinsic value.
  - M-03: buys are priced at spot moved against the buyer by the feed's deviation threshold (`setSpotBuffer`).
  - L-01 to L-04: band-aware strike rounding, proposals judged at the epoch-open snapshot, no emergency cancel once a price is recorded, protocol mandate floors.
  - I-02, I-03: no reputation feedback on transferred identities; keeper sigma changes capped at 25% per hour.
